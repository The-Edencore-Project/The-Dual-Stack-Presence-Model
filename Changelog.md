## 🌿 **Versioning Philosophy**
DSPM evolves slowly and deliberately. Changes prioritise:
- clarity
- safety
- conceptual precision
- architectural integrity

No change will ever introduce:
- emotional intelligence
- personality
- symbolic behaviour
- cross‑stack influence
- sentience or simulated awareness

These boundaries are permanent.
