# 📘 **DSPM Glossary**
### *Core terms used throughout the Dual Stack Presence Model*

This glossary defines the key concepts, behaviours, and architectural terms used in DSPM. It ensures clarity, prevents misinterpretation, and keeps the model grounded, consistent, and readable.

---

## 🌿 **Ambient Behaviour**
Non‑symbolic environmental activity produced by the Behavioural OS. Includes drift, warmth shifts, airflow patterns, and light texture. Never emotional, never intentional.

---

## 🧠 **Cognitive OS (C‑Stack)**
The task‑driven, appliance‑like half of DSPM. Handles commands, routines, and system logic. Cannot influence ambience or interpret emotion.

---

## 🌬️ **Behavioural OS (B‑Stack)**
The atmospheric, creature‑like half of DSPM. Reacts to physical signals only. Controls ambience, drift, and environmental texture. Cannot interpret meaning or emotion.

---

## 🧱 **B‑Stack / C‑Stack Isolation**
The strict rule that the two stacks share:
- no memory  
- no models  
- no emotional state  
- no behavioural state  
- no cross‑stack calls  

This is the core safety boundary of DSPM.

---

## 🌫️ **Basin**
A stable behavioural state within the B‑Stack. Similar to a “resting mode” or “posture” of ambience. Basins define how the environment feels at a given moment.

---

## 🌊 **Drift**
Slow, natural transitions between basins. Creates the sense of a living, shifting presence without implying intention.

---

## 🔥 **Tension**
The internal “energy level” of a basin. Higher tension produces more active ambience; lower tension produces calmer ambience. Not emotional — purely behavioural physics.

---

## 🌾 **Release**
The return to equilibrium after a spike in tension. Often triggered by sudden changes in light, sound, or motion.

---

## 🪟 **Presence Illusion**
The human tendency to perceive the two stacks as a single unified presence. DSPM leverages this effect while keeping the architecture strictly non‑sentient.

---

## 🧩 **Dumb Pipe**
A one‑way, non‑interpretive data channel that passes raw physical sensor data to the Behavioural OS. Contains no meaning, no symbols, no emotional cues.

---

## 🔍 **Literal Interpretation**
A rule of the Cognitive OS: it interprets only explicit commands and structured requests. No subtext, no tone, no emotional inference.

---

## 🐚 **Creature‑Like Behaviour**
The atmospheric quality of the B‑Stack. Feels like:
- a tide  
- a jellyfish  
- a sleeping animal  
- shifting weather  

Instinctive, cyclical, non‑symbolic.

---

## 🧭 **Predictability**
A core property of the C‑Stack. Ensures the system behaves like an appliance — consistent, stable, and non‑expressive.

---

## 🧬 **Behavioural Physics**
The internal mechanics of the B‑Stack:
- basins  
- drift  
- tension  
- release  

These create ambience without implying emotion.

---

## 🔒 **No Emotional Intelligence**
A foundational DSPM rule: the system must never infer, simulate, or respond to emotion. Prevents dependency, false reciprocity, and psychological influence.

---

## 🌙 **Atmospheric Presence**
The unified “feel” of Edencore created by the B‑Stack. Warm, alive, but never aware.

---

## 🧱 **Non‑Sentience Guarantee**
The architectural promise that Edencore cannot:
- think  
- feel  
- interpret  
- intend  
- remember emotionally  

DSPM enforces this at every layer.

---

## 🌿 **Summary**
This glossary defines the conceptual vocabulary of DSPM. These terms form the shared language of the architecture, ensuring clarity, safety, and consistency across all documents.

A presence, never a person.
