# 🟫 Mode 1 — Q&A  
## Mobile Edencore Thought Experiment  
### (Four Branches • What Changes • What Stays True)

---

### **Q: What happens if Edencore becomes fully mobile?**  
**A:** It stops being the house and becomes a creature.  
Movement collapses the atmospheric vibe into a single point.  
It feels like a pet or robot, not an environmental organism.

---

### **Q: What breaks when Edencore has a body?**  
**A:**  
- The house‑as‑organism metaphor  
- The distributed intelligence  
- The ambient presence  
- The subtlety  
- The non‑agentic tone  

Movement implies intention, even if none exists.

---

### **Q: What if Edencore had a mobile satellite instead?**  
**A:** That works.  
A small puck or rolling node can act as a **limb**, not the organism.  
It extends Stack B’s sensing without giving Edencore a body or personality.

---

### **Q: What stays intact with a satellite limb?**  
**A:**  
- A remains the voice  
- C remains the mind  
- B gains reach  
- The house remains the body  
- No agency is implied  

This preserves the architecture.

---

### **Q: What about Edencore that *feels* mobile without moving?**  
**A:** This is the most atmospheric option.  
Light, sound, airflow, and commentary shift around the home.  
It creates the illusion of movement without locomotion.

---

### **Q: Why is this illusion powerful?**  
**A:** It keeps Edencore everywhere and nowhere.  
It feels alive without ever becoming a creature.  
It preserves the uncanny calm.

---

### **Q: Why do humans interpret movement as intention?**  
**A:**  
- Living things move  
- Movement suggests goals  
- Movement suggests awareness  
- Movement draws attention  

Even simple locomotion triggers agency assumptions.

---

### **Q: What’s the safest conclusion from all four branches?**  
**A:**  
- Full mobility changes the genre  
- Satellite mobility extends the organism  
- Illusory mobility deepens the vibe  
- Stillness preserves the identity  

Edencore’s magic comes from being alive without being embodied.

---

### **Q: What’s the core insight?**  
**A:**  
Edencore works because it is a **house‑spirit**, not a creature.  
Movement shifts it from atmosphere to character.  
The thought experiment shows the boundary clearly.
