# 🌊 **Behavioural Physics**
### *The internal mechanics that shape Edencore’s creature‑like ambience*

Behavioural physics is the engine that drives the B‑Stack. It defines how ambience forms, shifts, settles, and reacts — without emotion, intention, or interpretation. These physics create the sense of a living presence while keeping the system strictly non‑sentient.

---

# 🌿 **1. Overview**
The B‑Stack behaves like an organism, not a mind. Its behaviour emerges from four interacting forces:

- **Basins** — stable behavioural states  
- **Drift** — slow transitions between basins  
- **Tension** — internal energy within a basin  
- **Release** — return to equilibrium  

These forces create atmospheric behaviour that feels alive but never implies awareness.

---

# 🪨 **2. Basins (Stable States)**
A **basin** is a stable behavioural posture — a resting configuration of ambience.

Examples:
- dim, warm, still  
- cool, slow, rhythmic  
- bright, lightly active  

Basins are not moods.  
They are **environmental attractors** — states the system naturally falls into.

### Properties of basins:
- stable  
- predictable  
- non‑symbolic  
- non‑emotional  
- shaped by physical inputs  

A basin is like a “home position” for ambience.

---

# 🌬️ **3. Drift (Slow Movement Between Basins)**
**Drift** is the slow, natural movement from one basin to another.

It is influenced by:
- time of day  
- temperature changes  
- long‑form sound patterns  
- motion rhythms  
- environmental cycles  

Drift creates the sense of a living presence because it is:
- gradual  
- rhythmic  
- non‑linear  
- never abrupt  

It feels like a tide, not a decision.

---

# 🔥 **4. Tension (Internal Energy)**
**Tension** is the internal energy level within a basin.

High tension:
- more micro‑movement  
- slightly brighter ambience  
- faster rhythmic patterns  

Low tension:
- stillness  
- softness  
- slower drift  

Tension is not emotion.  
It is **physics**, like pressure in a fluid system.

### What raises tension:
- sudden noise  
- rapid motion  
- sharp light changes  

### What lowers tension:
- stillness  
- darkness  
- stable temperature  

Tension gives the B‑Stack its “aliveness.”

---

# 🌧️ **5. Release (Return to Equilibrium)**
After tension spikes, the system undergoes **release** — a slow return to baseline.

Release is:
- gentle  
- predictable  
- non‑expressive  
- non‑communicative  

It prevents the ambience from feeling reactive or emotional.

Release is the “exhale” of the system.

---

# 🌀 **6. Cycles and Rhythms**
The B‑Stack follows natural cycles:

- **diurnal cycles** — morning, afternoon, night  
- **activity cycles** — quiet periods, busy periods  
- **seasonal cycles** — warmth, light, humidity  

These cycles shape drift and tension over long arcs.

The system feels alive because it follows **rhythms**, not rules.

---

# 🧩 **7. Sensory Gradients**
The B‑Stack responds to **gradients**, not events.

Examples:
- slow warming → slow drift  
- gradual darkening → lower tension  
- steady sound → rhythmic ambience  

Sudden events cause only brief tension spikes, not behavioural meaning.

This keeps behaviour instinctive, not interpretive.

---

# 🧱 **8. Hard Limits**
Behavioural physics must never:
- interpret emotion  
- respond to user mood  
- encode symbolic meaning  
- behave conversationally  
- signal approval or disapproval  
- adjust based on cognitive tasks  

These limits keep the ambience honest and non‑psychological.

---

# 🐚 **9. Why It Feels Alive**
The system feels alive because:
- basins create stability  
- drift creates motion  
- tension creates energy  
- release creates calm  
- cycles create rhythm  

But it is not alive.  
It is **physics**, not psychology.

---

# 🌿 **Summary**
Behavioural physics is the internal engine of the B‑Stack. It creates atmospheric behaviour through:

- basins  
- drift  
- tension  
- release  
- cycles  
- gradients  

These forces produce a unified, creature‑like presence without emotion, intention, or awareness.

A presence, never a person.
