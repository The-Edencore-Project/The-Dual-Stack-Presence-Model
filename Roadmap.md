# 🗺️ **DSPM Roadmap**
### *How the Dual Stack Presence Model will grow, stabilise, and mature over time*

This roadmap outlines the conceptual development of DSPM. It is not a product roadmap. It is a **theory roadmap** — a clear view of what is stable, what is evolving, and what remains open for exploration.

DSPM is intentionally minimal. Its evolution must preserve dignity, safety, and architectural clarity.

---

# 🌿 **1. Completed Foundations**
These components are stable and form the core of the model:

- **Philosophy.md** — the human‑centred foundation  
- **Behavioural-OS.md** — atmospheric, creature‑like layer  
- **Cognitive-OS.md** — task‑driven, appliance‑like layer  
- **Principles.md** — the non‑negotiable rules  
- **Glossary.md** — shared conceptual vocabulary  
- **README.md** — repo identity and overview  

These documents define the baseline architecture and will change only for clarity, not direction.

# 🔭 **4. Long‑Term Research Areas**
These are open questions — not commitments — that may shape future versions of DSPM:

### **A. Multi‑Room Presence Coherence**
How the B‑Stack behaves across multiple physical zones without implying intention.

### **B. Temporal Behavioural Rhythms**
Long‑scale behavioural cycles (day, week, season) that remain non‑emotional and non‑symbolic.

### **C. Sensory Expansion**
Exploring additional physical inputs (humidity, vibration, barometric pressure) while preserving non‑interpretive behaviour.

### **D. Formal Specification**
A more formalised, academic description of DSPM for research or publication contexts.

---

# 🧱 **5. Stability Commitments**
These aspects of DSPM will **never** change:

- No emotional intelligence  
- No shared memory between stacks  
- No behavioural influence from cognition  
- No interpretation of meaning in the B‑Stack  
- No personality, no character, no simulated empathy  
- No symbolic behaviour  
- No emotional reciprocity  

These are the pillars of the model.

---

# 🪶 **6. Versioning Approach**
DSPM will evolve through:

- **Minor revisions** — clarity, structure, terminology  
- **Major revisions** — only if a conceptual breakthrough occurs  
- **Changelog updates** — every meaningful change documented  

The model grows slowly and deliberately.

---

# 🌿 **Summary**
This roadmap guides the evolution of DSPM as a conceptual architecture. It preserves the core ethos:

- dignity  
- safety  
- clarity  
- non‑sentience  
- atmospheric presence  

DSPM will deepen, not drift.

A presence, never a person.
