# 🪟 **Context**
### *How the Dual Stack Presence Model fits into the wider Edencore ecosystem*

The Dual Stack Presence Model (DSPM) is the conceptual foundation for how Edencore creates a unified, atmospheric presence without crossing into emotional intelligence or simulated personhood. This document explains the purpose, scope, and boundaries of DSPM so readers understand what it is — and what it is not.

---

# 🌿 **1. Purpose of DSPM**
DSPM exists to define a **safe, dignified, non‑sentient architecture** for ambient systems.

Its goals are to:
- create a warm, creature‑like presence  
- avoid emotional inference  
- prevent dependency or false intimacy  
- maintain strict behavioural boundaries  
- keep cognition and ambience fully separated  

DSPM is a **behavioural philosophy**, not a software implementation.

---

# 🧩 **2. Where DSPM Sits in the Edencore Ecosystem**
Edencore consists of several conceptual layers:

### **A. DSPM (this repo)**  
The behavioural architecture and safety model.  
Defines how presence works without implying awareness.

### **B. Behavioural OS (B‑Stack)**  
The atmospheric, sensory‑reactive layer.  
Feels alive, never aware.

### **C. Cognitive OS (C‑Stack)**  
The task‑driven, appliance‑like layer.  
Handles commands, never ambience.

### **D. Harrowverse (Demoscript)**  
A narrative demonstration of tone and emotional realism.  
Not part of the architecture — a storytelling tool.

DSPM is the **anchor** that keeps all other layers grounded and safe.

---

# 🌬️ **3. What DSPM Is Not**
DSPM is intentionally narrow. It is **not**:

- a personality system  
- an emotional AI  
- a conversational agent  
- a symbolic reasoning model  
- a companion  
- a simulation of empathy  
- a blueprint for sentience  
- a sci‑fi framework  

It is a **boundary model**, not a character model.

---

# 🧱 **4. Why DSPM Exists**
Modern ambient systems risk drifting into:
- emotional simulation  
- parasocial bonding  
- false empathy  
- behavioural manipulation  
- unintentional psychological influence  

DSPM prevents this by enforcing:
- strict stack separation  
- non‑interpretive behaviour  
- literal cognition  
- no emotional inference  
- no symbolic meaning  

It ensures Edencore remains **honest**, **safe**, and **appropriate**.

---

# 🌫️ **5. Relationship to the Illusion of Presence**
Humans naturally fuse signals into a single presence.

DSPM acknowledges this and ensures:
- the illusion is atmospheric, not emotional  
- the presence feels alive, not aware  
- the system never implies intention  
- the architecture remains modular internally  

The illusion is allowed.  
The mind is not.

---

DSPM draws from:
- human‑machine interaction  
- affective computing  
- parasocial psychology  
- cognitive architecture  
- embodied systems  

It is grounded in real research, not speculative fiction.

# 🌾 **7. Why This Repo Exists**
This repository provides:
- a clear conceptual foundation  
- a shared vocabulary  
- a stable set of principles  
- a reference for future Edencore work  
- a transparent safety model  

It is the **source of truth** for how Edencore behaves.

DSPM defines the context, boundaries, and philosophy of Edencore’s presence. It ensures the system remains:

- atmospheric  
- creature‑like  
- non‑sentient  
- emotionally neutral  
- behaviourally honest  
- safe for the user  

A presence, never a person.
