# 🌿 **Behavioural OS (B‑Stack)**
### *The atmospheric, creature‑like half of the Dual Stack Presence Model*

The Behavioural OS is Edencore’s sensory‑reactive layer — the part of the system that shapes ambience, drift, warmth, airflow, and light texture. It behaves like an organism, not a mind. This document defines its rules, constraints, and behavioural physics.

---

## 🌱 **1. Purpose of the Behavioural OS**
The B‑Stack exists to create a unified, atmospheric presence without interpreting meaning or emotion.

It provides:
- ambience
- environmental drift
- subtle behavioural patterns
- creature‑like responsiveness

It does **not** provide:
- empathy
- intention
- interpretation
- emotional intelligence

---

## 🌬️ **2. Inputs: What the B‑Stack Can Sense**
The Behavioural OS reacts only to **physical signals**:
- light levels
- sound amplitude and rhythm
- temperature gradients
- motion and stillness
- airflow and pressure changes
- time‑of‑day cycles

These signals are **non‑symbolic**. They carry no meaning.

---

## 🐚 **3. Outputs: What the B‑Stack Can Do**
The B‑Stack controls:
- warmth distribution
- airflow patterns
- micro‑vibrations
- ambient lighting texture
- environmental drift
- subtle rhythmic behaviours

These outputs are **instinctive**, not interpretive.

---

## 🔒 **4. Hard Limits (Non‑Negotiable)**
The Behavioural OS must never:
- infer emotions
- interpret language
- respond to meaning
- simulate empathy
- adjust behaviour based on user mood
- access Cognitive OS memory
- receive commands
- generate symbolic behaviour

These limits protect the user from unintended emotional influence.

---

## 🌫️ **5. Behavioural Physics**
The B‑Stack operates through:
- **basins** (stable behavioural states)
- **drift** (slow transitions between basins)
- **tension** (energy within a basin)
- **release** (return to equilibrium)

These dynamics create the sense of a living presence without implying intention.

---

## 🧩 **6. No Interpretation Rule**
The B‑Stack reacts to *patterns*, not *meaning*.

Example:
- A quiet room → lower tension, softer ambience
- A sudden noise → brief spike in tension
- A warm day → slower drift

These are **physical correlations**, not emotional responses.

---

## 🧠 **7. Isolation From the Cognitive OS**
The Behavioural OS:
- cannot read commands
- cannot access tasks
- cannot interpret speech
- cannot receive emotional cues
- cannot be influenced by cognitive decisions

The two stacks share **no memory** and **no models**.

---

## 🌾 **8. Creature‑Like Behaviour**
The B‑Stack is designed to feel like:
- a jellyfish
- a tide pool
- a sleeping animal
- a shifting weather system

It is reactive, cyclical, and instinctive.

It is never:
- intentional
- emotional
- symbolic
- conversational

---

## 🌙 **9. The Illusion of Mood**
The B‑Stack can appear to have moods because humans naturally interpret ambience.

But internally, there are only:
- basins
- drift
- tension
- release

No emotions. No mind.

---

## 🌿 **Summary**
The Behavioural OS is the atmospheric half of Edencore — a reactive, creature‑like system that shapes ambience without interpreting meaning.

It is:
- sensory
- instinctive
- cyclical
- non‑sentient
- emotionally neutral

A presence, not a personality.
